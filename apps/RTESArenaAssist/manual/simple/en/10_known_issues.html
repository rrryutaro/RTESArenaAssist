Known Bugs & Cautions

These are known bugs in Arena itself (the game, not Assist) that tend to happen in normal play and can affect your progress or save data. They are bugs in the game, so Assist cannot fix them.

Before playing, read "How to save" and "Things you must not do". When something goes wrong, look up what you see on screen in "When something goes wrong".

How to save

  • Rotate between the save slots (there are 10). Do not keep overwriting a single slot.
  • If something seems wrong, do not overwrite your existing slots. Save to an empty slot.
  • Some bugs are fixed by quitting Arena, starting it again and loading (the game's temporary state was disturbed). Try this first when something is wrong. Damage that has been written into the save data is not fixed by restarting.

Things you must not do

The following actions can make things impossible to undo or badly break the game. Do not do them.
  1. Do not hand equipped armor or weapons to an equipment store for "Repair". Do not sell them. Always unequip first. Otherwise the game goes into a broken state (symptoms in the table below).
  2. Do not travel far while an item is in for repair. The game can only remember a limited number of towns and dungeons (roughly 8 to 16; large dungeons use more), and forgets the oldest first. It forgets the shop holding your item too, and the item is lost. Pick it up soon after it is ready.
  3. In the Main Quest, after delivering an item, do not go back to the dungeon where you found it and pick up the same item again. The same item is there again, but picking it up counts as getting the next stage's item, so the quest skips ahead. The skipped stages' destinations never appear on your map, so you cannot go there and cannot continue the Main Quest. This happens at every stage, starting with the dungeon you go to after the one you first escape from.
  4. Do not refuse a Main Quest request. If you refuse and then talk to the same person again, the game crashes, and you cannot continue the Main Quest from there. There is no time limit once you accept, so accept even if you want to do it later.
  5. While playing, do not choose "Start New Game" from the menu. It causes bugs: the start date is wrong, artifact quests never come, and state from the previous character (a disease, an escort, etc.) remains. To start a new game, restart Arena first and start without opening the load screen.
  6. Do not use Passwall to go into stairs or doors. You can get stuck in a wall or end up on a broken floor. If you save on a broken floor, the game cannot be continued.
  7. Do not take a palace quest without saving first. If you fail it, that palace may never give you quests again. To undo it you need a save from before you took the quest.
  8. Do not fight with bare hands. Do not break doors with bare hands. The game often crashes. Carry a weapon, even a weak one.
  9. Right after starting Arena, do not load a wilderness save and ask for "Rumors" in that wilderness. The game can crash. After starting Arena, first load a save made inside a town, or travel to a town once before asking.
